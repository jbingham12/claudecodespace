What kind of vacation is this?

The show looks like a luxury postcard and behaves like a pressure cooker. The fun is watching tiny frictions—status, money, desire, guilt, entitlement—turn the air conditioning down to “haunted.”

🍹

Paradise, slightly curdled

Bright pools, soft towels, and a feeling that the resort brochure left out several emotional hazards.

🛎️

Service with a subtext

Staff and guests orbit each other in a system where politeness is mandatory and power is never really off-duty.

📱

Modern anxieties

Careers, relationships, identity, family, and self-image all arrive with luggage. Some of it is overweight.

🌊

Comic dread

It is funny, uncomfortable, and very good at making silence feel like a chandelier about to fall.
