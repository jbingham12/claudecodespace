Off-grid solar, made visible.

A tiny educational sandbox showing how the same 3 kW PV array and 10 kWh battery behaves across synthetic summer and winter cabin-load scenarios.

Security note: no private, confidential, client, transcript, or proprietary material was used. This is a public-safe educational artifact with synthetic assumptions.

Interactive 72-hour trace

Switch seasons to see PV generation, load, battery state of charge, unmet load, and curtailed energy.

What it says

Summer overproduces: reliability hits 100%, but much of the PV is curtailed after the battery fills. Winter is the stress case: load rises, solar hours shrink, and 8.65 kWh goes unmet over three synthetic days.
